## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Sidebar Suggest a Package button opens Google Form
Clicking "Suggest a Package" in the sidebar SHALL open the Google Form in a new tab.

#### Scenario: Button click
- **WHEN** user clicks "Suggest a Package"
- **THEN** the Google Form SHALL open in a new browser tab

### Requirement: Footer Submit it here link opens Google Form
Clicking "Submit it here" in the footer SHALL open the same Google Form in a new tab.

#### Scenario: Link click
- **WHEN** user clicks "Submit it here" in the footer
- **THEN** the Google Form SHALL open in a new browser tab
